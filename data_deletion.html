Data Deletion Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

At Flowy-Motivation & Affirmation, we respect your privacy and data autonomy. Because our application does not require user registration or account creation, we do not store directly identifiable personal profile data on our servers.

1. Data Associated with Subscriptions (RevenueCat & App Stores)

If you have purchased a subscription through the Apple App Store (managed securely via RevenueCat), your transaction data and purchase receipts are handled directly by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not have access to your credit card information or personal identity.

2. Analytics and Backend Data (PostHog & Supabase)

We use third-party services like PostHog (for product analytics and onboarding survey insights) and Supabase (for core backend database synchronization). These services may collect anonymous or device-associated usage metrics to help improve app functionality. Because no personal user accounts exist, these records are tied only to anonymous installation IDs.

3. How to Stop Data Collection and Delete App Data

Since no account is required to use the App, you can completely remove any local data and stop all tracking by taking the following steps:

  • Uninstall the App: Delete Flowy-Motivation & Affirmation from your iOS device. This immediately removes all local app preferences, cached data, and stops future analytics tracking from your device.
  • Cancel Active Subscriptions: If you have an active auto-renewing subscription, you must cancel it directly through your Apple ID subscription settings to stop future billing. Neither we nor RevenueCat can cancel subscriptions on your behalf.
  • Request Data Purge: If you wish to request the removal of any anonymous analytics or onboarding data associated with your device ID from our systems (PostHog/Supabase), you can contact us directly via email with your device or support details.
