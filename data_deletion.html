Data Deletion Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

At Flowy-Motivation&Affirmation, we respect your privacy and data autonomy. Because our application does not require user registration or account creation, we do not store personal profile data on our servers.

1. Data Associated with Subscriptions (RevenueCat & App Stores)

If you have purchased a subscription through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store (managed securely via RevenueCat), your transaction data and purchase receipts are handled directly by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat. We do not have access to your credit card information or personal identity.

2. How to Stop Data Collection and Delete App Data

Since no account is required to use the App, you can completely remove any local data or association by taking the following steps:

  • Uninstall the App: Delete Flowy-Motivation&Affirmation from your device. This immediately removes all local app preferences and cached data.
  • Cancel Active Subscriptions: If you have an active subscription, you must cancel it directly through your Apple ID or Google Play Store subscription settings to stop future billing. Neither we nor RevenueCat can cancel subscriptions on your behalf.
